Agents that actually act

I build agentic AI systems — software that calls tools, reads real data, and acts on it, instead of just answering questions in a chat window. Reading a Gmail inbox, routing a WhatsApp conversation, looking up a Shopify order: the agent decides what to do and does it, inside guardrails a human set.

Production discipline matters more to me than a clever demo. Typed schemas, a human-approval gate before anything sends, cost tracking, retry logic, proper error handling — every agent I build carries all of it, because an agent that's occasionally wrong and always confident is worse than one that never shipped.

I also take on CAD and finite element analysis work on the side — mostly medical and orthopedic hardware, from implant design to structural simulation — background from an engineering degree that still comes in useful.
